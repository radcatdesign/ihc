IHEARTCOMIX
One word, all caps! Nice to meet you.

IHEARTCOMIX is a Los Angeles based creative marketing agency and content studio with a focus on entertainment, music, and lifestyle brands. Our specialty is imagining original concepts, executing spectacular productions and speaking to this coveted audience. We are experts in experiential, first-to-market ideas, digital strategy and fully immersive campaigns. We lead with a creative and community-first approach generating award winning activations and original IP.

Since our inception the IHEARTCOMIX brand has had a reputation for being at the forefront of emerging trends and dreaming up groundbreaking concepts, all steeped in a deep love of media. We have been a key player in the evolution of the Los Angeles creative scene, helping generate the unique fusion of music, art and culture that currently exists. It's this history that gives us a singular identity and an unmistakable energy that's earned IHC a trusted credibility.
